FAQ

How long does campaign activation take?

Campaigns are automatically activated 1.5 hours after creation.

How do payment approvals work?

Payment requests are reviewed by our team and typically processed within 24 hours.

What are the package tiers?

Standard ($0-300), Bronze ($301-1000), Silver ($1001-3000), Gold ($3001-8000), Platinum ($8001-25000), Diamond ($25001+). Higher tiers unlock better campaign performance.

Can I change my email address?

Yes, you can update your email in the Profile section.
